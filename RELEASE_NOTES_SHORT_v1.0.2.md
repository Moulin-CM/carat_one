# CaratOne Invoice Generator - Version 1.0.2 Release Notes

## What's New

### ✨ Email Invoices Directly
Send invoices via email with PDF attachments. Pre-filled emails with professional templates make it easy to share invoices with clients.

### 🔔 Smart Invoice Reminders
Never miss a payment deadline! Set custom reminders for invoice due dates and receive notifications even when the app is closed. Visual indicators show overdue, upcoming, and due-soon reminders.
